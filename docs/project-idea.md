We plan to create a 2D survival game in Unity where the player will be terraforming planets to become hospitable to human life. The player will be sent to planets one by one, using nothing but their spaceship and their surroundings to accomplish tasks that will make the planet livable. They will also have to deal with a variety of world biomes from arid deserts to freezing tundra, with the climates gradually becoming harsher and more volatile as the player’s terraforming efforts gain more renown.
